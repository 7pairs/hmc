# Project Instructions

## Japanese Documents

- Use the `yomiyasu` skill when creating a Japanese-language document. Apply the skill before delivering the document.
- Ordinary chat messages do not require `yomiyasu`.
- Treat Japanese-language document drafts shared in chat as documents. Apply `yomiyasu` before posting the draft.

### Style Rules for Japanese Documents

Apply these rules to new Japanese documents for hmc and to the parts of existing Japanese documents you edit.

- Use half-width Latin letters and digits in prose. Separate labels from their values with one ASCII space. Preserve the original typography of official titles and verbatim quotations, and of example inputs and logs where the exact characters matter.
- Separate inline code from surrounding prose with one ASCII space. Omit the space at the start or end of a line, next to punctuation, and immediately inside parentheses.
- Place supplementary links at the end of a paragraph or after the last sentence in a table cell. Put them immediately after the final Japanese full stop, as in `。（参照：[Document title](path/to/document.md)）`. Separate multiple links within the same parentheses with `・`. Keep links that form part of a sentence in the sentence.
